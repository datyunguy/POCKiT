Coupons get lost in screenshots, emails and chats

Save Your Savings gives every coupon a home, a deadline and a way to be used.

  • Keep every code in one place

    Paste a code and a link. For popular stores, the brand and category fill in for you, and you can add the minimum spend and terms.

  • Get reminded before it expires

    Reminders arrive 7, 3 and 1 days before expiry, and on the day. The savings closest to expiring sit at the top of your list.

  • Turn used savings into rewards

    Mark a coupon as used or exchange it for SaveCoins, then spend your coins on rewards. In this demo the rewards are simulated.

How it works

  1. Save a coupon

    Add the code, the store link and the expiry date.

  2. See what is expiring

    Your wallet shows active, expiring soon and used savings.

  3. Use it or exchange it

    Open the store from the app, copy the code, then mark it as used.
